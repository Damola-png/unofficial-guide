===== QUERY =====
What should a CS internship resume include?


=== Grounded Answer ===
A CS internship resume should include relevant skills, such as software expertise and programming languages [S3]. It's also important to be concise and clear, structuring the resume well to provide the information recruiters want [S5]. Additionally, the resume should target the specific career, in this case, a software engineering internship [S5]. It's recommended to ditch the "objective" section unless you're right out of college or want to transition to a new role [S3, S5]. A summary section, if used, should be filled with key accomplishments backed up by hard numbers [S3].
