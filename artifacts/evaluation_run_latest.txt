===== QUERY =====
What should a CS internship resume include?


=== Grounded Answer ===
A CS internship resume should include skills such as software expertise, programming languages, and other applicable skills [S3]. It's also important to be concise and structure the resume well, providing the information recruiters want [S5]. Additionally, the resume should target the career, in this case, a software engineering internship, and not include irrelevant information [S5]. It's recommended to ditch the "objective" section unless right out of college or transitioning to a new role [S3, S5].

Do referrals help compared to cold applications?


=== Grounded Answer ===
Referrals are implied to be more effective than cold applications, as the context suggests being grateful and taking a referrer for coffee, implying a personal connection is beneficial [S1]. In contrast, cold calling is described as "spammy" [S1], suggesting it may be less effective. However, the context does not provide a direct comparison of the two methods' success rates.
